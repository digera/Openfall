┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│                                                                │
│  Name             K   D   Dealt  Taken                         │

│  --- TIDE ---                                                  │

│                                                                │
│  --- VERDANT ---                                               │
│  Wisp-17         11   2   5120    980                          │
│  Wisp-22          6   5   2640   2210                          │
│  Player-19        3   9   1890   3450                          │
│                                                                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

FEATURES:
- Semi-transparent dark background (repeated dark chars)
- Title in bright white
- Column headers in muted grey
- Team headers in team colors (EMBER red, TIDE blue, VERDANT green)
- Player names in light grey
- Local player highlighted in bright yellow
- Stats show as integers/floats
- Sorted by team, then kills (descending)
- Appears instantly on Tab press, disappears on Tab release

DISPLAY NAMES:
- Currently uses entity_display_name() which returns:
  - "Player-XX" for human players (where XX is entity ID)
  - "Wisp-XX" for bots
- When PR #26 (typed names) lands, these will be replaced with
  actual player-chosen names automatically through the same helper

NO SPECTATORS:
- Only active combatants are shown
- If spectators exist, they won't appear (as per requirements)
