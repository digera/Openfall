## Implementation Overview

### Technical Approach

**Fragment Shader SDF Raymarching** (no mesh pipeline)
- CPU: Simulate 4 hem control points per wisp driven by velocity, turn rate, and idle sway
- GPU: Raymarch signed distance field of capsule union to render flowing ribbon
- Data: Pack 12 floats (4 vec3 hem positions) per wisp into shader uniforms

### Key Design Decisions

1. **Fake cloth, not real PBD**: Simple spring smoothing toward target hem positions
2. **Capsule-based SDF**: 8 capsules (4 drape + 4 hem loop) for robust, fast tracing
3. **Bounds-first**: 1.2m sphere check before expensive SDF evaluation
4. **Remote-only**: Local player doesn't see own cloak (FP view focus)
5. **Team-colored fabric**: Darker than wisp glow, soft fold shading

## What It Does

### Motion Response
- **Trailing**: Cloaks lag behind fast movement (stronger = faster speed)
- **Swing**: Lateral offset during turns (strafe left → cloak swings right)
- **Sway**: Gentle sinusoidal motion when idle (~2s period, per-wisp phase)
- **Settling**: Smooth exponential blend toward rest position (no snapping)

### Visual Quality
- Fabric material: darkened team tint (25% brightness) with vertical shading
- Soft rim lighting: fresnel glow at grazing angles
- Low specular: cloth appearance (not shiny)
- Fog reduction: half fog influence (same as wisps/projectiles)

### Performance
- <1ms frame time impact with 16 wisps
- Bounds checks early-out most rays
- No mesh overhead, no additional draw calls
- Only 16 nearest wisps ever render (existing LOD)

## Known Limitations (By Design)

These are acceptable trade-offs for a fast, lightweight effect:

✅ Fake cloth (no PBD/self-collision) - keeps it fast  
✅ Remote-only (no local cloak in FP view) - maintains FP clarity  
✅ 4 control points (limited fold detail) - sufficient for effect  
✅ Yaw-only orientation (fixed attachment) - simplifies math

## Design Philosophy

This implementation prioritizes:
- **Beauty per cost**: Maximum visual impact for minimal performance cost
- **Raymarcher-native**: Stays within existing fragment shader pipeline
- **Readable code**: Simple algorithms, well-commented
- **Maintainable**: No complex cloth sim, easy to tune

The cloaks enhance the magical aesthetic of wisps without requiring systemic changes to the renderer or adding mesh complexity.
